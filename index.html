About Me

I build systems,
not just products.

I'm a product-focused Product Manager who works at the intersection of strategy, clarity, and execution. My core belief is simple: most early-stage teams don't fail because they lack talent — they fail because everyone is building a different version of the same product.

My work is about eliminating that gap. I translate founder vision into clear documentation, defined ownership, and structured execution — giving cross-functional teams the alignment they need to move fast without breaking things.

I sharpened this approach through the Zedu PM Internship, where I navigated real team dynamics, developer drop-offs, API integration challenges, and the full weight of shipping a product under pressure.

Case Study 01 · Zedu Desktop Team

Keeping a Product Alive When the Team Is Falling Apart

The Problem

Seven developers dropped off within one week. Frontend and backend teams were misaligned. API integrations were breaking. The product was at risk of stalling completely.

What I Did

Maintained momentum by re-establishing communication structures, documenting agreed standards, and working cross-functionally to keep all remaining team members oriented around the same goal.

The Outcome

The product shipped. The team remained functional. The experience reinforced that a PM's highest value in a crisis is clarity — not just effort.

Case Study 02 · Product Thinking

Building a Country-Specific Product Concept from Scratch

The Challenge

Develop a viable product concept tailored to a specific country's market context — with full user workflow and strategic justification.

My Approach

Led with user research to understand local pain points, mapped a detailed user workflow, and aligned the product concept to both user needs and business viability.

Key Takeaway

Strong product thinking is not about having all the answers — it is about asking the right questions in the right order and letting user insight drive structure.
